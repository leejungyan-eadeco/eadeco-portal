The Business Portal does routine NAV work for the companies EADEPRO handles. Today that means **recurring invoices**: invoices that repeat on a schedule (daily, weekly, monthly…), which the portal creates in NAV as **drafts** for someone to review and post.

## Signing in

- Use your normal **Windows (EADECO) username and password**.
- You stay signed in for **1 day**.
- You need to be on the portal's user list. If you see *"You don't have access yet"*, ask an admin to invite you.

## What this page shows

| Box | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Invoices due in 7 days** | Recurring invoices that will be created in NAV in the next week, and their total |
| **Failed runs** | Drafts the portal could not create in NAV and has not managed to create since |
| **Active recurring invoices** | How many are running; paused ones are counted separately |
| **Companies set up** | Companies IT has added to the portal |

**Coming up in the next 14 days** lists what will be created next. **Needs attention** lists failures still open; open **Activity** to see what NAV said.

## Good to know

- The portal **never posts** anything. Every invoice it makes is a **draft** in NAV.
- It never creates **two drafts** for the same recurring invoice on the same date.
